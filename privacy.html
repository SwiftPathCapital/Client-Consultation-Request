PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: May 12, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 12, 2026

SwiftPath Capital ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") prioritizes your privacy. This policy details how we manage information collected when you access swiftpathtocapital.com or engage with our consultation forms for professional advisory or financial services.

1. Collected Information

We collect specific personal details when you interact with our website inquiry modules:

  • Contact name, Business entity, Corporate email, Phone number, Capital requirements and files, Preferred engagement windows

Our systems also log technical data including IP addresses and browser configurations via standard analytics to help improve user experience.

2. Utilization of Data

We process your provided data to:

  • Fulfill consultation requests and deliver business advisory communications via phone, SMS, or AI-generated voice technology (upon consent)
  • Enhance platform functionality
  • Maintain legal compliance and prevent fraudulent activities

3. SMS and Mobile Data

Phone numbers are collected solely for business communications regarding advisory and funding services. By sharing your number, you agree to contact via phone, SMS, and AI voice. You may opt out of mobile messaging at any time.

  • Frequency of messages varies
  • Standard data rates could apply
  • Revoke consent at any time by texting STOP to any message received
  • Text HELP for assistance or email submissions@swiftpathtocapital.com
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes
  • All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties

4. Information Disclosure

We do not trade, sell, or rent user data. We only disclose information to third parties under the following strict and limited conditions:

  • To vetted service providers supporting our operations under confidentiality mandates
  • To comply with lawful subpoenas or government requests
  • To protect the safety, property, or legal rights of SwiftPath Capital and our clients

5. Data Retention

We store your information only for the duration required to satisfy the business purposes defined here or to comply with statutory requirements.

6. Cookies and Tracking

This site utilizes cookies to optimize browsing. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies, though this may impact functionality of certain website features.

7. External Links

Our platform may link to outside websites. We hold no responsibility for their privacy standards.

8. Protection of Minors

Our services are designed for adult business professionals. We do not intentionally gather data from individuals under 18 years of age.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the specific personal data we maintain about you
  • Request the correction or deletion of your records
  • Opt out of SMS and voice communications by replying STOP

Direct all inquiries to submissions@swiftpathtocapital.com

10. Policy Amendments

We may modify this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated versions will be posted here with a new effective date.
